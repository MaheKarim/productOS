About Me

I'm a product manager who believes that great products are built on great metrics. For the past 8 years, I've helped B2B and consumer companies turn data into growth.

My approach is simple: understand the problem deeply, measure what matters, test systematically, and scale what works. I've done this across 23 products, from 0-to-1 launches to scaling products to millions of users.

What sets me apart is the combination of strategic thinking and hands-on execution. I don't just recommend what to build—I roll up my sleeves and build it with the team, whether that's defining metrics, designing experiments, or optimizing conversion funnels.

Outside of product work, I build tools and frameworks that help other PMs make better decisions. You'll find many of them on this site, free to use.
